# Thriftesque team guide

For the people who run the shop. Everything here happens at **https://thriftesque.com/login**, on a phone or a laptop.

## Signing in

Use the team email and password you were given. Signing up isn't possible on purpose: new team logins are added by Nuvai.
Forgot the password? Ask Nuvai to reset it.

## How customers order

1. They add pieces to their bag. That doesn't hold a piece, so two people can want the same one.
2. They tap "Send order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with a ready-made message: pieces, codes, sizes, prices, delivery fee,
   total, their name and area.
3. You reply on WhatsApp to confirm. The first confirmed order gets the piece.

## Add a piece

1. **+ Add product**.
2. **Add photos**: up to 4. The first is the cover. Photos are cropped to 4:5 and made small automatically. A plain,
   contrasting background works best.
3. **Make cut-out from cover** for the outfit builder and looks. The first time it downloads a model (44 MB), then it's quick.
   If parts are missing, long-press the piece in your phone's Photos app ("lift subject"), save it, and use **Upload your own
   cut-out**.
4. **Fill from photo** (optional): the AI suggests the name, category, era, colour, description and styling tip. Check every
   field; it can be wrong. Price, size, condition and measurements are always yours to fill in.
5. Fill in size, price (QAR) and the flat measurements in cm. The measurement boxes change with the category.
6. **Visibility**: "Live in shop" to publish now, or "Hidden (draft)" to finish later.
7. **Add to shop**. Each piece gets its code (TQ-0001, TQ-0002, ...) automatically.

## When someone orders

In the product list, change the piece's status:

| Status | Use it when | In the shop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live | It's for sale | Can be added to bags |
| Reserved | Someone asked and payment is pending | Shows "Reserved", can't be added |
| Sold | Paid | Shows "Sold", can't be added |
| Hidden | Draft, or taken down | Not shown at all |

The shop updates within a few seconds of a change.

## Shop settings

At the bottom of the dashboard: the WhatsApp number orders go to (country code first, digits only, like 97455551234), the
drop name shown at the top of the shop, and the delivery areas with their fees. Press **Save settings**.

## Looks

**Suggest looks** asks the AI for outfits from live pieces that have cut-outs. Suggestions arrive as drafts; nothing shows in
the shop until you press **Publish**. A published look hides itself if one of its pieces is sold or hidden.

## Good to know

- Deleting a piece also deletes its photos. It can't be undone; marking it Sold or Hidden can.
- Photos are made small on purpose (under 80 KB each) to keep the site fast and free to run.
- If something looks wrong, message Nuvai with a screenshot and the product code.
