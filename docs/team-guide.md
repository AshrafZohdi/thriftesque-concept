# Thriftesque team guide

For the people who run the shop. Everything here happens at **https://thriftesque.com/login**, on a phone or a laptop.

## Signing in

Use the team email and password you were given. Signing up isn't possible on purpose: new team logins are added by Nuvai.
Forgot the password? Ask Nuvai to reset it.

## How customers order

1. They add pieces to their bag. That doesn't hold a piece, so two people can want the same one.
2. They tap "Send order on WhatsApp". WhatsApp opens with a ready-made message: pieces, codes, sizes, prices, delivery fee,
   total, their name and area.
3. You reply on WhatsApp to confirm. The first confirmed order gets the piece.

## Add a piece

1. **+ Add product**.
2. **Add photos**: up to 4. The first is the cover. Photos are cropped to 4:5 and made small automatically. A plain,
   contrasting background works best.
3. **Make cut-out from cover** for the outfit builder and looks. The first time it downloads a model (44 MB), then it's quick.
   If parts are missing, long-press the piece in your phone's Photos app ("lift subject"), save it, and use **Upload your own
   cut-out**.
4. **Fill from photo** (optional): the AI suggests the name, category, era, colour, description and styling tip. Check every
   field; it can be wrong. Price, size, condition and measurements are always yours to fill in.
5. Fill in size, price (QAR) and the flat measurements in cm. The measurement boxes change with the category.
   **On sale?** Put the old price in "Price before the sale" and the new one in Price. The shop crosses out the old price and
   shows the % off. Empty it to end the sale.
   **Sections**: tick the sections the piece belongs to (see Sections below).
6. **Visibility**: "Live in shop" to publish now, or "Hidden (draft)" to finish later.
7. **Add to shop**. Each piece gets its code (TQ-0001, TQ-0002, ...) automatically.

## Add many pieces at once

1. **Bulk add**, then **Choose photos**: up to 30, one piece per photo (the cover shot of each).
2. Leave "Suggest names and descriptions" on to let the AI fill in each draft's name and description. Tick "Make cut-outs too"
   if you want the outfit cut-outs made now (slower).
3. **Add drafts**. Keep the screen open until it says it's done.
4. When it's done, the list shows each photo with what it still needs. Tap one to open it, add the price, size, condition,
   measurements and any extra photos, set Visibility to "Live in shop" and save. You land back on the list, ready for the
   next one. A piece can go live as soon as it has a name, a photo and a price; everything else is optional and simply left
   off the shop until you add it.
5. Drafts you don't finish now stay under **Hidden** in the product list, marked "Needs ...".

## Put many pieces live at once

Open the **Hidden** tab. Tick the pieces you want, or tick none to take all of them, then press **Make ... live** and tap it
again to confirm. Pieces without a price stay hidden (they show "Needs price").

## When someone orders

In the product list, change the piece's status:

| Status | Use it when | In the shop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live | It's for sale | Can be added to bags |
| Reserved | Someone asked and payment is pending | Shows "Reserved", can't be added |
| Sold | Paid | Shows "Sold", can't be added |
| Hidden | Draft, or taken down | Not shown at all |

The shop updates within a few seconds of a change.

## Shop settings

At the bottom of the dashboard: the WhatsApp number orders go to (country code first, digits only, like 97455551234), the
drop name shown at the top of the shop, and the delivery areas with their fees. Press **Save settings**.

**Terms and privacy details** (same panel): who runs the shop, a contact email, delivery, payment, returns, how long you hold
a reserved piece, and how long you keep order chats. They appear on the Terms and Privacy pages (delivery and payment also on
the shop). Anything you leave empty is simply left off those pages.

**About us** (same panel): the text of the About page. Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Until you write it, the page
shows a short default about the shop. Under it, **About page photos**: up to 8 (model shots, the team). They're cropped to
portrait (4:5), so keep the person in the middle. Add a short description to each if you can. Press **Save settings** to
publish them.

## Sections

Groups like Basics, Jackets or Men's, shown as buttons above the shop. Add, rename, reorder or remove them in the **Sections**
panel and press **Save sections**, then tick them on each piece in the editor. A piece can be in several. A section only shows
in the shop once it has a piece. Removing a section doesn't delete its pieces. Pieces on sale appear under **Sale**
automatically.

## Cut-outs

The outfit builder and looks only use pieces that have a cut-out (the piece with its background removed). If some pieces
don't have one, the dashboard shows a **Cut-outs** section: press **Make cut-outs** on a laptop and keep the page open. Then
check a few in the shop's outfit builder; if one looks wrong, open that piece and use "Upload your own cut-out" with your
phone's "lift subject".

## Looks

**Suggest looks** asks the AI for outfits from live pieces that have cut-outs, using whatever is in stock: tops with bottoms
when there are bottoms, layered tops (a top with a knit or jacket over it) when there aren't. Suggestions arrive as drafts; nothing shows in
the shop until you press **Publish**. A published look hides itself if one of its pieces is sold or hidden.

## Links for ads and posts

Shop links can open with a search already filled in: add `?q=` and the words. For example
`https://thriftesque.com/?q=bags`, `?q=jeans` or `?q=sale`. Spaces work as `+`: `?q=black+bag`.

## Good to know

- Deleting a piece also deletes its photos. It can't be undone; marking it Sold or Hidden can.
- Photos are made small on purpose (under 80 KB each) to keep the site fast and free to run.
- If something looks wrong, message Nuvai with a screenshot and the product code.
