Thriftesque: design directions

Each option is a working shop page: filter by category, open a piece, add it to the bag and build the WhatsApp order. They share the same products, copy and ordering flow. Only the look changes.

Sample products, the WhatsApp number, delivery fees and claims like "New drop every Thursday, 8 pm" are placeholders until the shop confirms them. Each option takes the feel of a reference site; none copies its logo, text or photos.
